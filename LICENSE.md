## Notes for the curious

**This is not an open-source license** as the Open Source Initiative
defines the term, because it restricts who may use the Software. It is
*source-available*: you can read all of it, and most readers can also
use all of it. Calling it open source would be inaccurate, so it is
not called that anywhere.

**The thresholds are borrowed rather than invented.** A hundred people
and a million dollars are the numbers the PolyForm Small Business
license uses, and using the same ones means a company that has already
decided how it feels about that license has already decided how it
feels about this one.

**Why revenue and headcount rather than one of them.** A ten-person
company making forty million dollars is not small, and a
three-hundred-person co-operative making eight hundred thousand is not
large in the way this cares about. Either test alone gets one of those
wrong.
