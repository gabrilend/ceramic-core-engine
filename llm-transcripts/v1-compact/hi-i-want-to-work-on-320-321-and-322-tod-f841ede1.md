- Mutator's "+1 hold" on refcount is the subtle bit: without it, the freer could observe refcount==0 between the swap and the mutator's own decrement. The hold means the mutator is itself a refcount holder, so the box record only frees AFTER the mutator's work happens-before whatever frees it.
